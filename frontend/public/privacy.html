OCP Market

Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated 24 October 2026 · 中文说明

1. About this service

This policy applies to the OCP Market virtual trading simulation at market.ocp-protocol.org, maintained by the OCP Market project team. The simulator lets players explore stock-related topics, Genesis price discovery, market positions and Core staking using virtual funds. It does not execute real stock trades or accept deposits or withdrawals. Other OCP services are outside this policy's scope.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in data: Clerk processes authentication. If you choose Google sign-in when enabled, Google and Clerk process the basic identity information you authorize, such as an account identifier, email address, name and profile image. Our application database stores your Clerk user identifier, verified email address and display name; it does not copy a Google profile image into the trading ledger.
  • Simulation data: virtual balance, the one-time virtual-currency grant, positions, market questions and outcome rules, stakes, trades, settlement claims, timestamps, and records used to prevent duplicate transaction processing.
  • Technical data: hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request timestamps, error and security logs. Authentication uses essential cookies and browser storage. Market pages currently load Google Fonts, which causes your browser to contact Google for font delivery.

For Google sign-in, the requested permissions are basic identity scopes: openid, email and profile. OCP Market does not request access to Gmail messages, Google contacts, Google Drive files or your Google password.

Google sign-in data flow

Google sign-in is not currently offered in the market interface. When offered, it is used only to sign in to your OCP Market simulation account. Google supplies basic identity to Clerk; Clerk manages authentication and provides a verified account identity to OCP Market. The application database stores the Clerk user identifier, verified primary email and display name. Our trading backend does not store Google access or refresh tokens and does not call Gmail, Drive, Calendar or Contacts APIs.

  • Account identifier and verified email: associate your signed-in account with the correct simulation wallet and positions, and validate account-data requests.
  • Display name: show your account name in your own signed-in account interface.
  • Basic profile image: may be handled by Google and Clerk during authentication; it is not copied into our trading ledger.
  • Access to private records: authorized project administrators may access the minimum necessary account records to resolve support, security or deletion requests. Public market visitors do not receive your Google identity or email.

3. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, maintain your simulation account, issue the initial virtual balance once, save and display your holdings, process simulated trades and settlement, prevent duplicate requests and abuse, and investigate service errors. Google user data is used for these user-facing account functions. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising or data-broker services, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

OCP Market's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the app's user-facing account functions. The app does not provide AI image generation or use Google APIs to create non-consensual intimate imagery.

4. Public information

Market questions, YES/NO/INVALID definitions, aggregate market state, prices and simulation activity may be visible to visitors without sign-in. Public activity identifies actors with player labels; it does not publish their sign-in email addresses. Do not put personal information into public market text. Your own balance and position information is returned to your authenticated account.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use Clerk for authentication, Neon for database storage, and Vercel for hosting and server functions. Google processes Google sign-in and font-delivery requests under its own policies. These providers process data to operate the service and may process it in countries other than yours. We may disclose necessary records when required by applicable law or to investigate misuse and protect the service. We do not disclose private account information as public market data.

6. Storage and security

Production connections use HTTPS. The application verifies sign-in sessions on the server and keeps authentication secrets and database credentials on the server rather than in browser code. Database records and service access credentials are available to authorized project administrators and the service providers needed to operate the application. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

Account details and simulation records remain stored while needed to provide your account, maintain the shared simulation and address security or abuse issues; the current simulator has no scheduled automatic account-data purge. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the contact below from your registered address. Requests are handled manually after verifying account control. Personal information that is no longer needed is deleted or de-identified. Public market records and the minimum records necessary to resolve security or integrity issues may be retained; if a deletion request has a limitation, we will explain it. Provider backup copies follow the provider's retention lifecycle.

You can revoke Google access from your Google account's third-party connections settings. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete the OCP Market database records; contact us to request their deletion.

8. Contact and changes

For OCP Market privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, contact cypherpunk.bc@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, authentication codes or client secrets. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.

中文说明

服务与收集的数据

本政策适用于 OCP Market 项目团队维护的 market.ocp-protocol.org 虚拟交易模拟，不涵盖其他 OCP 服务。模拟包含话题、Genesis、市场交易与 Core，使用虚拟资金，不进行真实股票买卖、充值或提现。

Clerk 处理登录。启用 Google 登录后，Google 与 Clerk 按你的授权处理基本账号信息，如账号标识、邮箱、姓名及头像。OCP 的数据库保存 Clerk 用户标识、已验证邮箱和显示名称，不把 Google 头像复制到交易账本。我们保存虚拟余额、首次赠金、持仓、话题与结果定义、质押、交易、领取、时间及防止重复操作的记录。基础设施提供商可能处理 IP、浏览器和设备信息、请求时间及安全日志；登录使用必要的 Cookie 和浏览器存储。市场页面目前使用 Google Fonts，浏览器会向 Google 请求字体。

Google 登录数据流程

市场界面当前暂未提供 Google 登录。若启用，Google 向 Clerk 提供基本身份信息，Clerk 处理认证，再向 OCP Market 提供经验证的账户身份。交易数据库保存 Clerk 标识、已验证主邮箱和显示名称，交易后端不保存 Google 访问令牌或刷新令牌，也不调用 Gmail、Drive、Calendar 或 Contacts API。邮箱和账户标识用于匹配模拟钱包、持仓及核实数据申请；显示名称用于你自己的已登录账户界面。Google 或 Clerk 可能处理头像，但交易账本不复制头像。授权管理员可为处理支持、安全及删除申请查看必要记录，公开访客不会获得你的 Google 身份或邮箱。

从 Google API 获得的信息，其使用和转移遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括 Limited Use 要求。不为与提供或改进用户可见账户功能无关的用途转移 Google 用户数据。本应用不提供 AI 图片生成，也不使用 Google API 制作未经同意的私密影像。

用途与公开范围

数据用于登录、保存模拟账户和持仓、仅发放一次初始虚拟资金、执行模拟交易与结算、防重复请求、防滥用及排查故障。Google 登录仅请求 openid、邮箱和基本资料，不读取 Gmail 邮件、联系人、云盘文件或 Google 密码。Google 用户数据不用于出售、定向广告、数据经纪服务或训练通用 AI 模型。

话题、YES/NO/INVALID 定义、市场汇总状态、价格和模拟操作可能公开展示；公开操作使用玩家编号，不公开登录邮箱。请不要在话题文字中填写个人信息。你自己的余额和持仓返回给你已登录的账户。

第三方、保护与保留

我们使用 Clerk 提供身份验证、Neon 保存数据库、Vercel 提供网页及服务端运行环境；Google 处理 Google 登录和字体请求。相关服务可能在你所在国家以外处理数据。为依法履行义务或调查滥用，可能披露必要记录。

正式环境使用 HTTPS，登录凭据在服务端校验，服务端密钥和数据库凭据不放进浏览器代码。运营所需的授权管理员和基础设施服务商能够接触必要数据；任何网络服务都无法保证绝对安全。

账户资料和模拟记录在提供账户、维护共享市场及处理安全问题所需期间保留，当前没有定期自动清空账户数据的机制。你可以用注册邮箱向下方地址申请查阅、更正或删除，经核实账户控制权后人工处理。不再需要的个人资料会删除或去标识化；公共市场记录及处理安全、账目完整性问题所需的最少记录可能保留，限制会向申请人说明。备份副本按服务商生命周期处理。撤销 Google 授权不会自动删除 OCP 数据库资料，删除资料需要另行申请。
