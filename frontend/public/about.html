OCP Market

A multiplayer virtual trading simulation for stock-related topics.

OCP Market is operated by the OCP Market project team. Players create a topic, define YES, NO and INVALID outcomes, trade gToken during Genesis, and participate in market trading and Core staking after graduation. Core produces the simulation outcome used for settlement.

Each new simulation account receives 100,000 virtual USD once. All balances, stock-token references, positions and rewards in this version are simulated. There are no real stock trades, deposits or withdrawals. OCP Market is not a brokerage and is not affiliated with the companies referenced in player topics.

What you can do

  • Browse public topics, outcome definitions, market progress and simulation activity without signing in.
  • Sign in to create topics, trade virtual positions, stake virtual funds and claim simulation payouts.
  • Keep your virtual balance and positions across visits and participate in the same markets as other players.

Why the app uses account information

Account information identifies your simulation account, saves your balance and positions, and prevents the same account from receiving its initial virtual funds repeatedly. Email verification is currently available. Google sign-in is not currently offered in the market interface.

If Google sign-in is enabled, it is intended only for basic identity: OpenID Connect account identification, verified email, and basic profile information. Google and Clerk handle authentication; the application ledger stores your Clerk account identifier, verified email and display name. The application does not need your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or Google password.

The application does not provide AI image generation or use Google APIs to generate non-consensual intimate imagery. Google user data is not used to train generalized AI models, sold, or used for targeted advertising.

OCP Market 是什么

OCP Market 是由 OCP Market 项目团队运营的多人虚拟交易游戏。玩家围绕股票相关话题定义 YES、NO、INVALID 条件，在 Genesis 买卖 gToken，毕业后参与正式市场交易与 Core 质押，并按 Core 产生的模拟结果结算。

每个新模拟账户首次获得100,000虚拟USD。本版本的资金、股票代币、持仓及奖励均为模拟，不进行真实股票买卖、充值或提现，也不提供证券经纪服务。页面提及的公司不代表与其存在合作或隶属关系。

为什么需要登录

浏览话题、市场进度、公开操作、应用介绍及隐私政策无需登录。创建话题、买卖、质押及领取模拟款项需要登录，以保存你的余额和持仓，并防止同一账户重复领取初始虚拟资金。

当前界面提供邮箱验证，暂未提供 Google 登录。若启用 Google 登录，仅用于基本身份验证，由 Google 与 Clerk 处理；应用账本保存 Clerk 账户标识、已验证邮箱及显示名称，不读取 Gmail 邮件、Google 云盘、联系人或 Google 密码。

本应用不提供 AI 图片生成功能，不使用 Google API 生成未经同意的私密影像；Google 用户数据不用于训练通用 AI、出售或定向广告。详细的数据处理、共享、保留和删除方式见隐私政策。联系邮箱：cypherpunk.bc@gmail.com。
